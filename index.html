Sweden's grid batteries doubled in 2025

Grid-connected batteries in Sweden grew from 551 MW to 1,128 MW in one year, according to official statistics. Most of the growth came from large battery parks.

Where the growth came from

Batteries holding more than 10 MWh each — mostly the large parks built to balance the grid — went from 280 MW to 666 MW. No single battery in the statistics held more than 100 MWh by the end of 2025.

About the numbers

The figures come from the table Grid connected batteries by storage capacity (TAB6631), published by Statistics Sweden for the Swedish Energy Agency and last updated 8 October 2026. Size classes group batteries by how much energy each one stores.

Statistics Sweden notes that both power and storage capacity are likely underreported, since this is a new and fast-growing area. Treat the totals as a minimum. Class values may not add up to the total because of rounding.

The statistics do not name individual sites. Batteries that came online in 2026, such as the 70 MW / 160 MWh park in Ånge, are not included yet.
