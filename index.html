About the list

Each project is checked against at least one public source: a company press release, an annual report, a project page or a news article. The project name links to the main source. Where sources disagree, the most recent figure is used and the disagreement is noted.

Operating means a source confirms the battery is commissioned. Under construction means construction has started but the battery is not confirmed online. Planned means an investment decision or building permit, and Permitting means a permit application is still being decided.

The list is not complete. Green Power Sweden counted 130 approved battery parks totalling about 5,100 MW at the end of 2025, most without a published developer or size. Projects are added as they are announced.
